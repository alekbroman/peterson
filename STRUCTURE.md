# Peterson Excavating & Landscaping Website Structure

## Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the website's organization and the role of each page. Detailed layouts, visual hierarchy, components, and responsive behavior will be developed during the design process.

## Primary Goals

The website should help visitors:

- Understand what Peterson Excavating & Landscaping does
- See the quality and range of its work
- Determine whether the company serves their needs and location
- Call, text, or request an estimate
- Find material, pickup, and delivery information

## Main Navigation

- Home
- Services
- Materials and Delivery
- Projects
- Service Area
- Why Peterson
- Request an Estimate

Request an Estimate should be the primary call to action. Calling and texting should also remain easy to access, especially on mobile.

## Home

The Home page should introduce Peterson Excavating & Landscaping, summarize its capabilities, demonstrate the quality of its work, and direct visitors toward the appropriate next step.

The opening hero will use an authentic project video that starts automatically without sound, plays inline, and loops continuously. The hero title will be **Local Experience. Lasting Results.**

The rest of the page should provide a high-level introduction to complete site work, visual access to the company's main service categories, examples of completed work, trust-building company information, customer reviews, an introduction to materials and delivery, and a clear estimate call to action.

## Services

The Services page should provide a clear overview of Peterson Excavating & Landscaping's work and direct visitors to individual pages for each major service. Each individual page should explain the service, show relevant work, and lead visitors toward requesting an estimate.

The main service categories are:

- Complete Site Work
- Foundations and Slabs
- Septic and Utilities
- Driveways
- Clearing and Stumping
- Boulder Retaining Walls

The site should not suggest that Peterson Excavating & Landscaping offers landscaping, concrete work, block retaining walls, or septic design.

## Materials and Delivery

The Materials and Delivery page should serve customers looking for aggregate materials. It should explain available materials, pickup, delivery, ordering, and relevant service-area information once those details are confirmed. Current base material prices should be listed openly on the page when approved and maintained.

The page should include a dedicated material-order and delivery-request flow rather than using the excavation estimate form. It should also offer a real-time quote for the complete order price, including materials and delivery, based on the customer's delivery address, selected material, quantity, and other required order details.

The result should be presented as a nonbinding quote with a clear disclaimer that the final price may change after confirmation. Possible factors include actual quantity, material availability, delivery access, route conditions, fuel costs, taxes, and other project-specific circumstances. The final pricing rules and disclaimer language must be approved before implementation.

After viewing the quote, the customer should be able to submit the order details as a request. Submission does not confirm the order or collect payment. Peterson Excavating & Landscaping must review availability, delivery conditions, and final pricing before accepting the order.

## Projects

The Projects page should be a gallery-style overview that uses authentic photography to demonstrate the quality and range of completed work. Projects may be organized by service to help visitors find examples relevant to their needs. Individual project pages are not needed at launch but may be added later for projects with enough useful detail and supporting media.

## Service Area

The Service Area page should help visitors determine whether their project is within Peterson Excavating & Landscaping's coverage area. It should include a map with an outline of the regions served, supported by clear written descriptions of the primary communities and approximate service boundaries. The same general coverage area applies to excavation work and material delivery. The map and wording should avoid implying rigid coverage limits unless those boundaries have been confirmed.

## Why Peterson

The Why Peterson page should build trust through the company's local roots, industry experience, family background, approach to workmanship, and approved credentials. It should include an Equipment and Capabilities section with the company's confirmed equipment list. Relevant service pages may reference specific equipment when it helps explain a capability, but the complete list should remain on the Why Peterson page.

## Request an Estimate

The Request an Estimate page should make calling, texting, and submitting an estimate request straightforward. It should also provide the company's general contact information. The page should explain that most excavation estimates require a site visit and allow customers to provide project details and relevant files.

## Shared Site Elements

The site should use consistent navigation, contact access, estimate calls to action, and footer information across all pages. Approved customer reviews should be distributed across relevant pages, including Home, Services, Projects, and Why Peterson, rather than placed on a separate Reviews page. Final treatment of these elements will be determined during design.
