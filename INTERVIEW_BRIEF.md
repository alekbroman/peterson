# Peterson Excavating & Landscaping Website Brief

## Website Goal

Create a modern, credible website that clearly explains what Peterson Excavating & Landscaping does, shows the quality of its work, and makes it easy for qualified customers to call, text, or request an estimate.

## Business Priorities

- Emphasize complete site work for new construction, including clearing, stumping, driveways, foundation excavation or slab preparation, septic installation, utilities, and finish preparation.
- Serve homeowners, contractors, and light commercial customers.
- Support aggregate pickup and delivery.
- Present quality workmanship as the main differentiator, supported by real project photos, reviews, and credentials.

## Services to Feature

- Complete excavation and site preparation
- Foundation and basement excavation
- Slab preparation
- Driveways
- Septic system installation and replacement
- Water and sewer service lines
- Underground infrastructure
- Tree clearing, stumping, and tree removal
- Large natural-rock and boulder retaining walls
- Topsoil placement and site finishing
- Aggregate pickup and delivery

Preliminary aggregate products include pit-run gravel, Class 5, crushed rock, trap rock, riprap, and boulders. The final product list and sizes still need to be supplied.

## Services Not to Promote

- General or detailed landscaping
- Patios, sidewalks, pavers, or concrete work
- Planting and shrubbery
- Block retaining walls
- Septic system design

## Service Area

The approximate service limits discussed were Two Harbors, Cloquet, and Cotton. Superior should not be listed. Final service-area wording should be confirmed before launch.

## Contact and Estimate Process

- Make calling and texting the primary contact options.
- Use Trish's phone number as the main public contact.
- Send website form submissions directly to Trish's regular email.
- Allow customers to attach photos, plans, septic designs, and other documents.
- Explain that most excavation estimates require a site visit.
- Do not publish equipment hourly rates. Trucking may sometimes be billed hourly, but no public rate was approved.

## Website Content and Features

- Clear service descriptions
- High-quality, authentic project photos and short videos
- Project gallery organized by service
- Selected Google reviews and a link for leaving a review
- Equipment and capabilities list
- NPCA information, septic license details, insurance information, and other approved credentials
- Company history covering Jason Peterson's local roots, lifelong industry experience, and family background in trucking and excavation
- Dedicated materials page with pickup, delivery, and ordering information

Only authentic, safe, and professionally appropriate project images should be used. Images showing unsafe or questionable conditions should be removed rather than altered with AI.
